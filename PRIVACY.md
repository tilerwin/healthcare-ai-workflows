# Privacy and Data Use

This repository is intended for educational, research, and portfolio purposes.

## Data Policy

- No real patient names or identifying information will be included.
- No protected health information will be uploaded.
- All example transcripts and patient records will be fictional, synthetic, or fully de-identified.
- Clinical documentation generated through experiments must be reviewed by a qualified clinician.
- Public AI tools should not receive protected health information unless an organization has completed the appropriate privacy, security, contractual, and compliance review.

## Design Principle

AI should reduce administrative burden while preserving patient trust, clinician oversight, and professional accountability.
