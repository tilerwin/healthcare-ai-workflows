# Healthcare AI Workflows

This repository documents my exploration of responsible, AI-assisted systems designed to reduce administrative burden in outpatient healthcare.

## Mission

Help clinicians spend less time documenting and more time caring for patients.

As a Doctor of Student Chiropractic candidate and chiropractic assistant, I have seen how documentation, billing, patient communication, and other administrative responsibilities can reduce the time clinicians have available for direct patient care.

My goal is to explore how AI can support these responsibilities while preserving clinician oversight, patient privacy, and professional accountability.

## Current Projects

### [01 — SOAP Automation](./01-SOAP-Automation)

Designing a workflow that transforms clinical encounter transcriptions into structured SOAP note drafts for clinician review.

### [02 — Patient Education](./02-Patient-Education)

Exploring the creation of personalized exercise handouts, visit summaries, and educational materials based on clinician-approved treatment plans.

### [03 — Appointment Follow-Up](./03-Appointment-Follow-Up)

Planning workflows for appointment reminders, progress check-ins, home exercise reminders, and post-visit communication.

### [04 — Workflow Diagrams](./04-Workflow-Diagrams)

Documenting how information could move through an AI-assisted clinical workflow, from patient intake through clinician review and follow-up.

### [05 — Future Roadmap](./05-Future-Roadmap)

Tracking short-term development goals, long-term ideas, ethical considerations, and future prototype plans.

## Guiding Principles

- Clinicians remain responsible for all final documentation and decisions.
- AI should support clinical work, not replace professional judgment.
- Patient privacy and informed consent must be considered from the beginning.
- No real patient information should be placed in public repositories.
- Outputs should be reviewed for accuracy before use.

## Current Status

This project is currently in the research, planning, and early prototyping stage.

No system in this repository is intended for independent clinical use, diagnosis, treatment, billing, or automated decision-making.
